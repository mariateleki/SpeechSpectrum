<p>Karen Davis appeared before the panel in support of her pardon application for convictions including assault, risk of injury, and violation of probation, stating that she takes full accountability for her actions. The panel noted that she has been off probation since 2019 and discussed her completion of services, including in-home therapy and anger management. Davis clarified that the in-home therapy was not mandated but offered as a resource, which she chose to accept because she wanted to break patterns from her upbringing, improve communication and stress management, and strengthen her relationship with her daughter; both she and her daughter participated together and separately. She also completed anger management, which she believed was connected to the assault charge. Regarding the serious assault offense, which involved a stabbing, Davis said she no longer has contact with the people involved and moved away from the area with her daughter. The panel also asked about alcohol, which Davis acknowledged played a role in both offenses; she said her current alcohol use is limited to infrequent social occasions. Davis further explained that she has been a member of her church since 2013 and has helped with service coordination, though her time is largely focused on work, school, and tutoring her son.</p>
