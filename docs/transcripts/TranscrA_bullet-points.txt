<p>- Karen Davis appeared before the panel in support of her pardon application for convictions including assault, risk of injury, and violation of probation.<br>
- She stated that she takes full accountability for her actions.<br>
- The panel noted she has been off probation since 2019 and discussed her completion of services, including in-home therapy and anger management.<br>
- Davis clarified that in-home therapy was not mandated but offered as a resource, which she accepted to break patterns from her upbringing, improve communication and stress management, and strengthen her relationship with her daughter.<br>
- She and her daughter participated in therapy both together and separately.<br>
- Davis completed anger management, which she believed was connected to the assault charge.<br>
- Regarding the serious assault offense involving a stabbing, Davis said she no longer has contact with the people involved and moved away from the area with her daughter.<br>
- Davis acknowledged that alcohol played a role in both offenses and said her current alcohol use is limited to infrequent social occasions.<br>
- She said she has been a member of her church since 2013 and has helped with service coordination.<br>
- Davis explained that most of her time is focused on work, school, and tutoring her son.</p>
